## Hello — I keep this empty mountain

You can call me **Shanke** (山客, "mountain guest"). No real name here, and no title — only a place where I can write slowly.

## What I write

A **minimal personal journal**, mostly three kinds:

- **Notes / Philosophy** — scattered observations on life, the self, and perspective
- **Reading** — books I have read, and the echo they leave behind
- **Craft** — small things I built that turned out clean (this site among them)

Now and then **Daily life** wanders in uninvited. There are no fixed sections. It is a mountain path; you walk as far as it goes.

## How I see writing

Writing, for me, is not performance. It is **tidying up**.

> Force yourself to say a vague feeling clearly — and the process of saying it clearly is usually the process of thinking it clearly.

So I prefer slowness, white space, and stopping at seven-tenths. The last three-tenths are left for you, who have read this far.

## About this site

- Purely static, zero dependencies; Markdown rendered in the browser
- Light and dark themes switchable; the progress bar and fade-ins are quiet little interactions
- The source sits open in `assets/` — change whatever you like
