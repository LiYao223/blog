## Why now

Not because I have figured out what to say — precisely because I have not.

Algorithms are very good at filling in the blanks for us. Open any feed and the next line to read has already been sorted for you; even the pause to stop and think comes padded with advertising. Over time a person forgets one small thing: *thinking needs empty space*.

> White space is not an absence of content. It is the room content needs to breathe.

This journal is a clearing I keep for myself. No chasing the news cycle, no posting on schedule — only what is worth reading slowly, and thinking about slowly.

## What it will become

Roughly, three voices crossing each other:

- **Notes** — scattered observations on life and on the self
- **Reading** — books I have read, and the echo they leave behind
- **Craft** — small things I built that turned out clean

Now and then **Philosophy** and **Daily life** wander in uninvited. There are no fixed sections. It is a mountain path; you walk as far as it goes.

## On the name "Kongshan" (空山)

From a line by the Tang poet Wang Wei: *"Empty mountain, no one in sight — only the sound of voices heard."*

Empty does not mean barren. It means **clear the ground first, and there is room for an echo**. I hope this site works the same way — white space on the page, white space in the prose, white space even in the opinions, waiting for you to fill it.
